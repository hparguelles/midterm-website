PHILIPPINE LITERATURE

Lualhati Bautista

Writer • Novelist • Activist • Political Critic

Lualhati Bautista was a Filipino writer whose literary works explored women's experiences, family, politics, social inequality, and Filipino identity.

Her stories connect the personal lives of Filipino characters with the larger social and political realities of the Philippines.
