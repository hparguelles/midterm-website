FEATURED LITERARY ANALYSIS

Dekada '70

A family story during a changing nation.

Dekada '70 is a novel by Lualhati Bautista that follows the Bartolome family during the politically turbulent 1970s.

Through Amanda Bartolome and her family, Bautista connects personal experiences with political and social changes in Philippine society.

Title

Dekada '70

Author

Lualhati Bautista

Context

Philippines, 1970s

Genre

Novel

LITERARY ELEMENTS

Themes

01

Women's Empowerment

Amanda's development shows her growing awareness of her identity and her desire to have a voice beyond traditional roles as a wife and mother.

02

Freedom

The novel explores personal and political freedom and shows how restrictions on society affect individual lives.

03

Family

The Bartolome family shows how political and social events can influence relationships inside the home.

04

Social Change

The younger generation challenges traditional beliefs and becomes increasingly aware of political and social issues.

CHARACTERS

The Bartolome Family

Amanda Bartolome

The central character. Her transformation represents a woman's awakening and search for independence.

Julian Bartolome

Amanda's husband who represents traditional patriarchal expectations within the family.

Jules Bartolome

Amanda's politically active son who represents youth activism and resistance.

Bartolome Family

Represents how national political events can affect ordinary Filipino families.

LITERARY ELEMENT

Conflict

01

Woman vs. Patriarchy

Amanda struggles against traditional expectations placed upon Filipino women.

02

Individual vs. Society

Characters experience pressure from the social and political environment.

03

Individual vs. Government

Political activism creates conflict between citizens and authority.

LITERARY ELEMENT

Symbolism

Amanda's Transformation

Amanda's transformation can be interpreted as a symbol of the awakening and increasing independence of Filipino women.

The Bartolome Family

The family can be interpreted as a reflection of Philippine society. Their internal conflicts mirror larger social and political tensions.

HISTORICAL & CULTURAL CONTEXT

The Philippines in the 1970s

01

Political Unrest

Political and social tensions shaped the experiences of many Filipinos.

02

Martial Law

Martial Law changed political and social life and affected freedom and activism.

03

Youth Activism

Young Filipinos became increasingly involved in political and social movements.

04

Women's Roles

Traditional expectations of women are questioned through Amanda's character.

FILIPINO IDENTITY

What Does It Mean to Be Filipino?

Dekada '70 presents Filipino identity through family, culture, gender roles, politics, and historical experience.

The novel shows that personal identity is connected to the society and history in which a person lives.
